## App Name

## Short Description

AI resume optimizer with ATS scoring and Word resume download.

## Full Description

ResumeFit Pro helps job seekers tailor their resume for a target job description. Upload a DOCX resume, paste the job description, review ATS score insights, missing keywords, suggested improvements, and download a professionally formatted Word resume.

Features:

- Resume upload and job description matching
- ATS score estimate
- Missing keyword analysis
- Resume improvement recommendations
- Optimized resume preview
- Word document download
- Installable web and mobile app experience
